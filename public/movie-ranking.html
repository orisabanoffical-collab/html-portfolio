The best movies according to Ori

My top 3 favorite movies of all time

1. Memento

A psychological thriller about a man with short-term memory loss who is trying to solve his wife's murder.

2. Interstellar

A science fiction film about a team of explorers who travel through a wormhole in space in an attempt to ensure humanity's survival.

3. Inglorious Basterds

A Tarantino film about a group of Jewish-American soldiers who are on a mission to kill Nazi leaders.
